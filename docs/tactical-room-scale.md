# Tactical room scale for Fortress

Select **Fortress**, then **Layout → Room scale → Tactical** in the dungeon generator. Standard is the default. The choice is saved; selecting another theme resets it to Standard. Surprise me and all other themes use Standard.

Tactical Fortress keeps the chosen room count and supports both five-room and ordinary one-to-four-floor layouts. Encounter rooms and the boss arena grow; main passages target three cells wide. Ordinary interior doors grow to two cells only where both rooms have floor beside the opening. Secret doors, stairs and entrances keep their original widths.

Each room reserves a route from its door and stair approaches toward open space near the center, with up to one extra cell on either side where the floor permits. Furnishings stay off those cells, and structural columns that would obstruct them are removed. Other columns and terrain remain as cover. On an upper floor constrained by the footprint below, a smaller boss room may remain after attempts to fit the larger arena.

Existing scenes need no migration. Standard generation keeps the same seeded output. Scene creation and image exports retain Atlas's image budgets, so larger maps may have fewer pixels per cell.

To check in Obsidian, generate a Fortress in five-room and multi-floor modes, create a scene, and inspect door widths, routes, walls, grid alignment and level links. Reopen the generator to confirm the saved choice; select another theme and return to confirm the reset.
