## New

**Draw and Text are one tool**
- The Draw tool has its own bars above the toolbar: Select, Pen, Line, Rectangle, Ellipse, Text, Stamp and Eraser, and beside them the tools of the mode in hand
- Every mode starts with the same four: a colour, a size (Small to Extra large, which fit the map's grid), Players see it, and On the map, which lays a drawing on the floor under the grid and the tokens
- Line has dashes, arrowheads, curves, a fill and see-through ink; Rectangle and Ellipse have dashes and a fill; Text has a font (Medieval, Sci-fi, Mono), a look, alignment, bold and italic; Stamp has its symbols
- With drawings or texts selected, the bars show their style and a pick changes them, in one undo step
- Select mode takes drawings and texts only, by a click, a marquee or a lasso: those on the map, hidden and locked ones too. Ctrl/Cmd+A selects all of them
- Select mode brings drawings to the front or sends them to the back, and locks them. A locked drawing or text stays as it is until you unlock it: it is not moved, restyled, erased or deleted, and a copy of it is not locked
- A selected drawing or text has a frame with handles: drag inside to move, a corner to resize, a side to stretch, the handle above to turn. Shift keeps the shape or turns in 15° steps
- A line made with Line shows its points: drag one, click the line to add one, double-click one or press Delete over it to remove it
- A shape, stamp or text you just made shows its handles until you press somewhere else, so one that came out a little short is pulled right at once. A pen stroke shows none, so the next stroke starts anywhere
- Texts join the marquee, the lasso, group moves and group turns, and Alt-drag leaves a copy of them
- Right-click a drawing or text for its menu: Edit (a text), Show to or Hide from players, On the map or Above the tokens, Lock, To front, To back, Duplicate, Delete. Double-click a text to edit it
- With the move and select tools, drawings on the map and locked ones stay put, and in every other tool a drawing or text takes no click
- Clear removes the drawings above the tokens, everything on the level, or what players drew
- B selects the tool and, pressed again, steps to the next mode. T selects it in Text mode. While you draw, E switches to the eraser and back, and , and . step the size
- The Text button left the toolbar: the Draw button writes too, and shows the mode in hand
- The pen steadies your hand and leaves a smooth line; hold Shift to go on in a straight piece
- Drag a line, a rectangle or an ellipse, or place it click by click. On a line, Space adds a corner, Backspace takes one back, Enter or a double click ends it, and ending on its first point closes it. Shift gives 15° steps, a square or a circle
- Shapes and stamps snap to the grid while Snap is on; hold Alt to place them freely. A stamp of medium size is one cell
- The eraser cuts a drawing exactly where it passes, through filled shapes too, and removes a text it touches. Hold Alt to remove whole drawings. One sweep is one undo step
- See-through ink has one even tone, also where a stroke crosses itself
- What you draw with Players see it switched off is yours alone: it is not in the player window, in Session View, in scene thumbnails or online
- A drawing on the map is lit like the floor: players see it only where they see or remember the map
- Texts and drawings lie in one order, so a text can stand above a drawing

**Fog tools**
- The fog tool has its own bars above the toolbar: Hide and Reveal, and beside them the shape, the brush's size, Cover all and Reveal all
- Cover all hides the whole map in one step, and Reveal all clears it. Undo takes either back
- [ and ] make the brush smaller and larger
- Hold Alt while you draw to do the other thing: reveal with Hide, hide with Reveal
- Escape drops the stroke you are drawing
- More sets how dark fog looks to you in GM View. Players always see solid fog

## Improved

- A lasso and a rectangle show the fog they will leave while you draw them
- The fog brush's ring shows on light maps and on dark ones
