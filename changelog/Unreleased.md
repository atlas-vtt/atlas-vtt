## Improved
- The update notice no longer interrupts prep or play: it waits until no map, player window or online table is open, and shows after a week at the latest
- TrueScale: the setup screen says what it is and which card to use (a bank or credit card, 85.6 × 54 mm), and the full screen button and the players' chip say what they do
- Player window: showing a saved frame glides there, also when players were following you, and changing the scene dips through black instead of jumping

## Fixed
- The confirmation for deleting a collection in Manage no longer opens behind the asset manager

- The player window can be moved by dragging it again
- Editing a value in the statblock beside a note no longer jumps away from what you are editing

- TrueScale: saved frames can be shown and removed every time. Hover a frame on the map for its chip; it stays on the visible part of a frame larger than your view. The bookmark lights up when a frame is saved, and a second click takes the save back instead of adding a copy.
- TrueScale: a moved Bring players here frame goes back to its place when a drag is cancelled, and no longer carries its position over to the next map.

- Picking or resetting a game system now shows its Stress bar on the scenes the collection already has
- Atlas now says so once when Obsidian Sync is set to leave out the files scenes and maps are made of, which kept them from reaching other devices
