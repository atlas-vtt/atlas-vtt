## Improved

- Wall corners and dragged points follow Snap to grid, including hex corners. Hold Alt to place or drag freely.
- New wall corners automatically join and split existing wall segments in the same undo step. Door ends can snap to existing wall points when resizing.

## New

- Place windows from a wall's context menu, or mark a door as a window. Windows have their own badges; open shutters let sight and light through, and closed shutters block them.
- Reveal discovered secret doors from their context menu so players see their door badges when in sight. Hide them again without changing the secret-door type.
