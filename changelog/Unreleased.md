## New
- Token sounds: give a token up to five sound effects from your vault, such as a dragon's roar or a battle cry, and play them during the game. Switch on Sound under Experimental features in the command palette, then add sounds in Edit Token under Sounds, each with its own volume and a button to hear it. Add sound lists the sounds you picked last first and the rest by folder, and typing a folder's name, such as "spells", finds the sounds in it. Then right-click the token and choose Play sound. Choosing a sound that is still playing starts it again, Stop sounds ends every token sound, and Stop all fades them out with the music. Sounds play on the GM's computer only, on the output chosen in Atlas settings, from MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, Opus, M4A, AAC and WebM files up to 3 minutes long.
