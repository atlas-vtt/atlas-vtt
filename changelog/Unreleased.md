## New

**New Feature: Sound**
- A Sound page on the DM screen for music and ambience, from your own folders in the vault or anywhere on disk
- Open the Sound page in a tab of its own from the command palette, with no map needed
- Moods: press one and the room crossfades to it, what both moods share keeps playing, and a mood is saved from whatever you have playing
- Up to 8 ambience layers beside the music, each with its own fader, and every track evened out in loudness
- Loops without a gap or a click, and Silence to fade it all and bring it back
- Link a mood to a scene or a fight and it plays by itself
- Online, your players hear it in time on their own devices, set their own levels and never see what a track is called

## Improved
- The update notice no longer interrupts prep or play: it waits until no map, player window or online table is open, and shows after a week at the latest
- TrueScale: the setup screen says what it is and which card to use (a bank or credit card, 85.6 × 54 mm), and the full screen button and the players' chip say what they do
- Player window: showing a saved frame glides there, also when players were following you, and changing the scene dips through black instead of jumping

## Fixed
- The player window can be moved by dragging it again
- TrueScale: saved frames can be shown and removed every time. Hover a frame on the map for its chip. The bookmark lights up when a frame is saved, and a second click takes the save back instead of adding a copy
- TrueScale: a moved Bring players here frame goes back to its place when a drag is cancelled, and no longer carries its position over to the next map
- Editing a value in the statblock beside a note no longer jumps away from what you are editing
- Picking or resetting a game system now shows its Stress bar on the scenes the collection already has
- Atlas now says so once when Obsidian Sync is set to leave out the files scenes and maps are made of, which kept them from reaching other devices
- The confirmation for deleting a collection in Manage no longer opens behind the asset manager
- Tags in a selection of several assets: a tag only some of them have now goes to all of them when pressed, and from all of them when pressed again
- Statblocks: a backslash keeps a comma in a label or word (`Fire\, cold: 3` shows as Fire, cold)
- Statblocks: rows added to a Counter block in a template now show on notes that already had some of its rows
