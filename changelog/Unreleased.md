## New

- Roll Atlas dice from anywhere. When viewing a note in Reading Mode, Atlas now detects any dice formula (2d4+3, +3, 1d20+5, etc.)
  allowing you to instantly roll with the Atlas dice roller. Fantasy Statblock dice roller format is also supported, allowing you to instantly roll from any Fantasy Statblock.

## Fixed

- On Windows, converting music or ambience in a linked folder now moves the originals to the trash when you chose that, instead of keeping them
