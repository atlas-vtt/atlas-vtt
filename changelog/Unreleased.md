## New

- Atlas Online: Sidekick puts the DM screen on your phone or tablet. Start it on the DM screen, open its link or QR code on the device and allow it with the number both show. It has three screens: your Stream Deck keys, the sound controls and the statblocks of the map in front

## Improved

- Atlas Online: players see your changes at once instead of up to a second later, such as Shared vision, remote mode, letting players draw, a character's dice and what your lighting shows them

## Fixed

- Atlas Online: on a map that plays by a game system preset you made, players see initiative grouped by that preset's sides, as on your tracker and the player window
- Stream Deck: Freeze and Curtain keys work at an online table without a player window, pausing and curtaining online players, and light up while they are on
- Initiative: taking the combatant whose turn it is out of the initiative, or deleting its token, now passes the turn by defeated combatants, as Next turn does
