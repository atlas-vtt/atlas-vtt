## New

- **Loop points for music**: right-click the waveform on the Sound page to set a track's loop in or loop out where you clicked, or give exact times from a library row's menu (Loop points…). Once the track plays on its own, it goes round between them, crossfaded at the seam. The loop shows as a band over the waveform; drag its edges to move it while the track plays. Hear the loop seam jumps to just before the seam, so you can check how it goes round.
- **Start at loop in**: a track can begin at its loop in instead of its start, set from the waveform's menu or a library row.
- **A track's own crossfade**: a crossfade dropdown in the music card's row sets how long the playing track crossfades into the next, and round its loop. A track that repeats on its own whole crossfades into its own start too. Auto keeps the setting's.
