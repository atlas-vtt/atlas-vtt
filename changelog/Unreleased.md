## New

**Sidekick**
- The fight list looks like the initiative tracker's compact view: each token in front, its art fading in behind, and its hit points filling red as it takes damage
- Change a creature's hit points and find its token on the map right from its statblock in Sidekick
- While a campaign is played, its world map shows faintly behind Sidekick's screens
- Statblocks slide in, a key's options move from step to step, and panels look like your phone's own
- Sidekick's panel in Atlas shows a bigger QR code, easier to scan from across the table
- A new Sidekick key opens and closes the player view
- Sidekick's QR code stays blurred until you click it, so it never shows on a stream
- The device list in Sidekick's panel stays closed until you open it
- On Sidekick's Sound screen, the levels sit above Now playing, away from where a thumb swipes

## Fixed

- On Samsung phones and tablets, a button you tapped no longer stays highlighted
- Sidekick on iPhone: token art in the fight list fills its row instead of showing the picture's empty edge
- Sidekick: a tap while the screens slide no longer changes a level or presses a key
- Initiative by cards: after a delayed combatant acts now, swapping two cards no longer gives the turn again to someone who already acted
- Laser pointer and grid alignment: the mouse cursor no longer turns back into an arrow while you move it over the map

## Atlas Online

- A player whose token waits beside the map sees "You" on its initiative card and is told when it is their turn
