## New

- **Rooms**: the lighting tool's new Rooms mode carves rooms out of solid rock instead of drawing walls one by one. Carve with a rectangle, corner by corner, a lasso or a brush, fill rock back in the same ways (Alt does the opposite, Shift snaps corners to the grid), and the outline of all open rooms stops sight, light, sound and tokens like walls. Drag a door across an opening and its ends reach the walls. The menu lists the level's rooms: highlight the one you carve into, start a new one, name, hide, split, join or delete them.
