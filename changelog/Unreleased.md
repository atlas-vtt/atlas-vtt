## New

- Atlas Online: Sidekick puts the DM screen on your phone or tablet. Start it on the DM screen, where you sign in to your Atlas account first if you are not yet, open its link or QR code on the device and allow it with the number both show. It has three screens: your Stream Deck keys, the sound controls and the statblocks of the map in front. In a fight the statblocks list it as your initiative tracker does, with the turn, the look-alike numbers and the sides, and can show only the players or only the NPCs. Press a roll in a statblock to roll it in Atlas as on the DM screen and see its result on the device; hit dice, which reroll your tokens' hit points, roll only on the DM screen

## Improved

- Atlas Online: players see your changes at once instead of up to a second later, such as Shared vision, remote mode, letting players draw, a character's dice and what your lighting shows them

## Fixed

- Atlas Online: on a map that plays by a game system preset you made, players see initiative grouped by that preset's sides, as on your tracker and the player window
- Stream Deck: Freeze and Curtain keys work at an online table without a player window, pausing and curtaining online players, and light up while they are on
- Stream Deck: at an online table without a player window, the handout key lights up while players see an image you showed them, and takes it back
- Initiative: taking the combatant whose turn it is out of the initiative, or deleting its token, now passes the turn by defeated combatants, as Next turn does, and when nobody else can act, the turn rests at the next combatant's place
- Initiative: delaying the last combatant in a fight by cards now deals the next round's cards, as Next turn does
