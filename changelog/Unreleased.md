## New

**New Feature: Sidekick (Atlas Online)**
- Your DM screen on a phone or tablet: a Stream Deck-style grid of keys, sound controls and the statblocks of your current map with initiative and rolls. Start it on the DM screen and scan the QR code

## Fixed

- Fixed bug where music would sometimes drops out while the Sound page was open
- Scroll bars in the music list, the library and the asset manager work again on older Obsidian installs
- Statblocks: counters on the DM screen and on hover cards update at once, and quick presses all count
- After a graphics driver crash, maps, lighting, fog, token art and 3D dice come back by themselves instead of staying black or blank
- Dice: numbers on coloured dice now have better contrast
- Faint text such as hints, counts and placeholders is easier to read in every look
- Medieval and Sci-fi looks: in source mode, only a bold word that opens a line uses the heading font
- Table log: roll totals are readable with any accent colour
- Appearance settings: dragging in the colour picker is smooth now
- Stream Deck: Freeze, Curtain and handout keys now work at an online table without a player window
- Initiative: removing the combatant whose turn it is passes the turn to the next one who can act
- Initiative by cards: a combatant's menu now offers Delay Turn and Act Now

## Atlas Online

- Changes to settings such as Shared vision or remote mode reach players at once instead of up to a second later
- Players see initiative grouped by sides on maps that use a game system preset you made
- Character sheet on a phone: Bonus Actions no longer shows as an empty Actions section
