## New

**Draw and Text are one tool**
- The Draw tool has its own bars above the toolbar: Select, Pen, Line, Rectangle, Ellipse, Text, Stamp, Cells and Eraser, and beside them the tools of the mode in hand
- Every mode that draws starts with the same four: a colour, a size (Small to Extra large, which fit the map's grid), Players see it, and On the map, which lays a drawing on the floor under the grid and the tokens
- Line has dashes, arrowheads, curves, a fill and see-through ink; Rectangle and Ellipse have dashes and a fill; Text has a font, a look, alignment, bold and italic; Stamp has its symbol; Cells has Fill and Unfill
- Stamps take every pin symbol, in any colour: places from a world map down to a room, doors, stairs, traps, treasure. Symbol opens them in their groups, with a search by name
- A stamp of medium size is one grid cell and sits on the cell; a small one is half a cell, large two and extra large three. Its frame turns it and makes it larger or smaller
- Stamps placed before keep their symbol
- Cells colours the grid's cells: drag across squares or hexes to fill them, and switch to Unfill to clear them again, whatever their colour. Cells of one colour that touch are one shape, with the grid's lines over it. One drag is one undo step
- Painting a cell another colour replaces the colour it had. A locked fill keeps its cells, and so does one on the other layer or one only you see
- Cells fills on the map unless you switch On the map off, and keeps that choice apart from the other modes'. On a map without a grid, or with the grid switched off, the mode is greyed out and says why
- A cell fill is a drawing like any other: select it, move it, hide it from players, erase into it, lock it
- Text is typed on the map, where it will stand: click and type for a text as wide as its words, or drag for a text with that width, whose words wrap. Enter finishes, Shift+Enter starts a new line, Escape puts back what the text said, and a text left empty is removed
- While you type, the bars change the text under your hands: its colour, size, font, look, alignment, bold and italic
- Three fonts: Medieval, Sci-fi and Mono
- Three looks: Outline gives the letters a thin halo, dark under light text and light under dark, so a label reads on any map without a box; Plate sets them on a dark chip; Plain is the letters alone
- With drawings or texts selected, the bars show their style and a pick changes them, in one undo step
- Select mode takes drawings and texts only, by a click, a marquee or a lasso: those on the map, hidden and locked ones too. Ctrl/Cmd+A selects all of them
- Select mode brings drawings to the front or sends them to the back, and locks them. A locked drawing or text stays as it is until you unlock it: it is not moved, restyled, erased or deleted, and a copy of it is not locked
- Group makes one of the drawings and texts you selected, in Select mode's tools and in the menu: a group is selected by a click on any of it, and moves, turns, resizes, hides, copies and deletes as one. Ungroup takes it apart again. A sketch cut by the eraser stays one group
- A selected drawing or text has a frame with handles: drag inside to move, a corner to resize, a side to stretch, the handle above to turn. Shift keeps the shape or turns in 15° steps
- A text's sides set the width its words wrap at; its corners make it larger or smaller
- A line made with Line shows its points: drag one, click the line to add one, double-click one or press Delete over it to remove it
- A shape, stamp or text you just made shows its handles until you press somewhere else, so one that came out a little short is pulled right at once. A pen stroke shows none, so the next stroke starts anywhere
- Texts join the marquee, the lasso, group moves and group turns, and Alt-drag leaves a copy of them
- Right-click a drawing or text for its menu: Edit (a text), Show to or Hide from players, On the map or Above the tokens, Lock, Group, Ungroup, To front, To back, Duplicate, Delete. Double-click a text, in the Draw tool or with the move tool, to type in it
- With only locked drawings selected, the tools that would change them are greyed out and say to unlock first
- With the move and select tools, drawings on the map and locked ones stay put, and in every other tool a drawing or text takes no click
- Clear removes the drawings above the tokens, everything on the level, or what players drew
- B selects the tool and, pressed again, steps to the next mode. T selects it in Text mode. While you draw, E switches to the eraser and back, and , and . step the size
- The Text button left the toolbar: the Draw button writes too, and shows the mode in hand
- The pen steadies your hand and leaves a smooth line; hold Shift to go on in a straight piece
- Drag a line, a rectangle or an ellipse, or place it click by click. On a line, Space adds a corner, Backspace takes one back, Enter or a double click ends it, and ending on its first point closes it. Shift gives 15° steps, a square or a circle
- Shapes and stamps snap to the grid while Snap is on; hold Alt to place them freely
- The eraser cuts a drawing exactly where it passes, through filled shapes too, and removes a text it touches. Hold Alt to remove whole drawings. One sweep is one undo step
- See-through ink has one even tone, also where a stroke crosses itself
- What you draw with Players see it switched off is yours alone: it is not in the player window, in Session View, in scene thumbnails or online
- A drawing on the map is lit like the floor: players see it only where they see or remember the map
- Texts and drawings lie in one order, so a text can stand above a drawing

**Fog tools**
- The fog tool has its own bars above the toolbar: Hide and Reveal, and beside them the shape, the brush's size, Cover all and Reveal all
- Cover all hides the whole map in one step, and Reveal all clears it. Undo takes either back
- , and . make the brush smaller and larger, as they size the Draw tool
- Hold Alt while you draw to do the other thing: reveal with Hide, hide with Reveal
- Escape drops the stroke you are drawing
- More sets how dark fog looks to you in GM View. Players always see solid fog
- Fog is changed with the fog tool only: with the select and move tools it is no longer selected, dragged, duplicated or deleted
- Polygon: click the corners of a shape. Enter, a double click or a click on the first corner closes it, Backspace or undo takes the last corner back
- Cells: drag over the grid to hide or reveal whole cells, squares and hexes alike
- Rectangles and polygons snap to the grid, to the ends of walls and along walls. A ring or a line shows what a point snapped to. Hold Ctrl or Cmd to place it freely
- Hold Shift for a square, or to keep a polygon's edge level, upright or diagonal
- Areas: draw the rooms of a map once, then reveal or hide each with one click on its badge, with any tool
- Choose Areas in the fog bar and draw a room with the rectangle, the polygon, the lasso or over cells. A new area ends where its neighbours begin, so areas never overlap
- Start a shape inside an area to add it to that area. Hold Alt to take it away from the areas it covers
- Click an area to select it. Drag a corner to reshape it, click an edge for a new corner, double-click a corner to remove it, drag inside the area to move it. Delete deletes it and Escape lets go
- Escape puts a corner or an area you are dragging back where it was
- An area's badge shows an eye while the area is revealed and a struck-through eye while it is hidden. Only you see the badges
- A revealed area's badge is smaller and fainter, and a token standing on it takes the click, so moving the party never hides a room. Badges take no click while the lighting tool is chosen
- Right-click a badge to reveal or hide its area, name it or delete it. Point at a badge to see the area's name and outline
- Cover all hides every area with the map and Reveal all reveals them. The areas stay, each one click from the other state
- Rectangles and polygons snap to the corners and edges of areas
- More deletes all areas
- Room: on a map with walls, click inside a room to hide or reveal it. The room under the pointer is outlined before you click
- A room ends at its walls and at every door, open or closed. Walls that stop only light, and windows, end none
- A corridor that runs all round other rooms is filled without them: the rooms inside stay as they are
- In Areas, a click with Room makes the room an area. A room that is an area already is selected instead
- More makes areas from walls: one area for every room the walls enclose, in one step. A room you already drew an area over most of gets none
- Fog fades: what you reveal fades away and new fog gathers, on your map and in the player window. With reduced motion it changes at once
- Hold H to see the fog as your players see it, solid and without the areas' badges, also on a map without lighting
- Party tokens clear fog: switch it on in More, and fog clears around the party's tokens as they are moved, as far as the distance you set. Walls stop it, open doors let it through
- It clears when a token is put down: at the end of a drag, when a token is placed on the map, and when a player moves their token online. Hidden tokens and opponents clear nothing
- Undo takes the move and the fog it cleared back together

## Improved

- On Windows and Linux, the window's minimise, maximise and close buttons stay out of sight over a map and the other Atlas pages, so the widgets in the top-right corner can be seen. Move the pointer to the corner to show them
- A shape shows the fog it will leave while you draw it. What it will reveal shows to you alone until you let go
- The fog brush's ring, the cell under the pointer and a polygon's corners show on light maps and on dark ones
- The fog rectangle snaps to the grid only while Snap is on

## Fixed

- Tokens and pins under fog can be clicked and dragged with the select and move tools: fog no longer takes their clicks

## Atlas Online

- Players may draw, a switch in the player view settings: your online players get a Draw tool of their own, with the pen, lines, shapes, text and an eraser. They change and erase only what they drew; you can change, erase and undo all of it, a drawing's menu says who drew it, and a player's entry in the dock clears theirs
- A map with more fog than players can be sent is shown covered to them, never open, and you are told
