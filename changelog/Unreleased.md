## Improved

- Atlas Online: players see your changes at once instead of up to a second later, such as Shared vision, remote mode, letting players draw, a character's dice and what your lighting shows them

## Fixed

- Sound: music no longer drops out for seconds at a time while the Sound page is open
- Scroll bars in Atlas (the music list, the library and the asset manager) follow the list again on Obsidian installs with an older Electron, and on the Sound page the music list's bar stays in the list and can be dragged
- Statblocks: a counter pressed on the DM screen or a token's hover card shows the change at once while its note is open
- Statblocks: quick presses on a counter all count
- Lighting: when the graphics driver crashes while a lit map loads, Atlas no longer says your graphics card cannot run the lighting, and the lighting comes back by itself once the driver is back
- Maps: after a graphics driver crash, the whole map comes back by itself instead of staying black, or black at its edges, until you open it again; so do the dynamic lighting's colours and tokens without art
- Maps: after a graphics driver crash, door badges, token icons and conditions, cell numbers and the fog show again by themselves instead of staying blank until the map is reopened
- Dice: numbers on coloured dice are easy to read again. Light dice get numbers in a deep shade of their own colour, dark dice keep their light numbers
- Dice: after a graphics driver crash the 3D dice roll with their numbers, colours and light again, instead of an empty panel, and the dice colours in Dice settings draw again
- Atlas' quieter text (hints, counts, placeholders, sleeping scene tabs, loot rarities, links) is easier to read and meets WCAG AA contrast with your own theme and in the Sci-fi look's dark scheme
- Medieval and Sci-fi looks: in source mode only the bold word that opens a line takes the heading font, as in reading view
- Table log: roll totals are written in the text colour instead of your accent colour, so they read well with a red or pale accent; critical rolls keep their colour
- Appearance settings and the welcome wizard: dragging in the colour picker for the accent or ivy colour is smooth again; Atlas takes the colour once you stop moving
- Stream Deck: Freeze and Curtain keys work at an online table without a player window, pausing and curtaining online players, and light up while they are on
- Stream Deck: at an online table without a player window, the handout key lights up while players see an image you showed them, and takes it back
- Initiative: taking the combatant whose turn it is out of the initiative, or deleting its token, now passes the turn by defeated combatants, as Next turn does, and when nobody else can act, the turn rests at the next combatant's place
- Initiative: delaying the last combatant in a fight by cards now deals the next round's cards, as Next turn does

## Atlas Online

- Sidekick puts the DM screen on your phone or tablet. Start it on the DM screen, where you sign in to your Atlas account first if you are not yet, open its link or QR code on the device and allow it with the number both show. It has three screens: your Stream Deck keys, the sound controls and the statblocks of the map in front. In a fight the statblocks list it as your initiative tracker does, with the turn, the look-alike numbers and the sides, and can show only the players or only the NPCs. Press a roll in a statblock to roll it in Atlas as on the DM screen and see its result on the device; hit dice, which reroll your tokens' hit points, roll only on the DM screen
- On a map that plays by a game system preset you made, players see initiative grouped by that preset's sides, as on your tracker and the player window
- On a phone, a sheet's tab group whose other tabs moved to Abilities shows the remaining tab under its own name, so Bonus Actions no longer looks like an empty Actions section
