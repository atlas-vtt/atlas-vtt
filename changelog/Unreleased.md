## New
- Token sounds: give a token up to five sound effects from your vault, such as a dragon's roar or a battle cry, and play them during the game. Add them in Edit Token under Sounds, each with its own volume and a button to hear it. Add sound lists the sounds you picked last first and the rest by folder, and typing a folder's name, such as "spells", finds the sounds in it. Then right-click the token and choose Play sound. Choosing a sound that is still playing starts it again, and Stop sounds ends every sound. Sounds play on the GM's computer only, from MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, Opus, FLAC, WebM and 3GP files up to 20 MB.

## Improved
- Edit Token stays where it opened while you edit: switching vision or a carried light on or off, or adding a sound, no longer moves the dialog, so the switch you clicked stays under the pointer.
