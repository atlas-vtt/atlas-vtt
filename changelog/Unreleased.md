## New

- **Soft and crackled fog edges**: Atlas settings, Maps, Fog edge fades the edge of the fog and of what you erase from it, or breaks it into a gritty, cracked edge that stays sharp at any zoom, over as many map pixels as you choose, for you and your players. Off by default. Contributed by ThatMattLatt

**New Feature: Sound**
- Sound, a page on the DM screen for music and ambience, from your own folders in the vault or anywhere on disk
- Open the Sound page in a tab of its own from the command palette, with no map needed
- Get starter ambience: six free ambient loops (forest, campfire, thunderstorm, beach, night marsh, cave) to play before you add your own
- Filter the library by tags: your folder names count as tags already, and a track can carry as many more as it needs
- Moods: press one and the room crossfades to it, what both moods share keeps playing, and a mood is saved from whatever you have playing
- Search (press /) narrows the moods beside the library as well as the tracks, and moods can be dragged into the order you want
- Up to 8 ambience layers beside the music, each with its own fader, and every track evened out in loudness
- Reverb on every layer and the music, from a knob above its fader, and each mood rings in its own space: a room, a hall, a cave or a cathedral
- Loops without a gap or a click, and Stop all to fade it all out and bring it back
- A queue for the music: tracks crossfade by a length you set, drag or Alt+arrows to reorder, repeat the list, one track or stop at the end, stop after this track, back, clear what's coming, and a list of what played
- Link a mood to a scene or a fight and it plays by itself
- Online, your players hear it in time on their own devices, set their own levels and never see what a track or mood is called or who made it

**Asset manager**
- An empty Tokens tab offers 155 free tokens, Oozejar's creatures (CC BY-SA 4.0) and class tokens in chalk, sorted into tags for you, with 5E (SRD 5.2.1) or Cairn statblocks attached if you like

## Improved

- Right-click a scene in the asset manager and choose Open Scene to open it, in addition to double-clicking it. Contributed by marinojoey

## Fixed

- The DM screen's text is sharp on screens without high resolution
- The choices of a "One of a list" value in a statblock, and of an ability's parts, now open in front of the card: in its last block they were cut off by its edge.
- A note previewed by hovering a link in a statblock preview (a spell, a condition) now opens in front of the statblock instead of behind it.
- Statblocks: emptying a linked statblock's hit points (or any other resource field), or setting them to 0, now removes the bar from tokens already on the map
- Deleting a map now also removes its image and thumbnail, unless another map or a scene still uses them.
- Renamed statblock notes no longer show up a second time under their old name when you link a statblock to a token
- Hit points a statblock template keeps under another name (such as `pv`) are no longer counted twice, as the hit points and as a tracked number of their own, so the DM screen, rests and the player sheet keep one value with its maximum
- Statblocks: a Counter row renamed in a template no longer shows twice on notes that already used it
- An enlarged text box can be rotated again: its rotate handle stays above the text, and both its handles follow it while you resize, rotate or undo.
- Settings opened from the command palette stay open instead of closing again right after a quick click
- Holding Shift places a note pin in the centre of a square, as it already did on hex grids
- With Show nameplates on in the player view, players see only the names your map shows: on the map, in their initiative list, turn banner and rolls
- Exported collections carry their random tables, with the notes they are read from
- Pins that roll a folder of encounters keep working after an import under another collection name
