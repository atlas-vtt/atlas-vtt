## Improved

**Sidekick**
- The fight list looks more like the initiative tracker's compact view
- Changing a Token's HP and finding it via the locate button in a statblock now works with your Sidekick
- While a campaign is played, its world map shows faintly behind screens
- Statblocks slide in, a key's options move from step to step, and panels look like your phone's own
- Sidekick's panel in Atlas shows a bigger QR code, easier to scan from across the table
- A new Sidekick key opens and closes the player view
- Sidekick's QR code stays blurred until you click it, so it never shows on stream
- Sound view: switched position of Levels and "Now playing" widgets so that on phones you don't accidentally changes volumes while swiping

## Fixed

- On Android phones and tablets, a button you tapped no longer stays highlighted
- Sidekick: a tap while the screens slide no longer changes a level or presses a key
- Initiative by cards: after a delayed combatant acts now, swapping two cards no longer gives the turn again to someone who already acted
- Laser pointer and grid alignment: the mouse cursor no longer turns back into an arrow while you move it over the map

## Atlas Online

- A player whose token waits beside the map sees "You" on its initiative card and is told when it is their turn again
