## Improved

- Rooms are now real walls: a room carved in the lighting tool's Rooms mode can be dragged by its points, deleted and given doors like any wall you drew
- Carving into a room opens into it and makes the two one room, whether that room was carved or drawn with walls; Fill in walls a room off again
- Rooms saved with 0.14.0 turn into walls when you open the scene. The Rooms panel is gone: joining two rooms is deleting the wall between them
- Fields, drop-downs and search bars in the asset manager and its dialogs have a quieter edge instead of a bright outline
